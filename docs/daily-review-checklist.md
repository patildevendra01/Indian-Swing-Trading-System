# Daily Swing Trading Review Checklist

## After market close
- [ ] Confirm scan data date and source; reject stale/incomplete data.
- [ ] Review NIFTY and relevant sector trend.
- [ ] Review candidate charts and liquidity; do not rely on scan ranking alone.
- [ ] Check NSE/BSE corporate announcements and company filings.
- [ ] Check upcoming results, board meetings, and other event risk.
- [ ] Confirm entry, structural stop, target, quantity, and at least 2R potential.
- [ ] Confirm planned risk is at most 0.5% of account capital.
- [ ] Record candidates rejected and why.

## Before the next session
- [ ] Re-check pre-open gap and current quote; do not blindly use yesterday's close.
- [ ] Recalculate quantity if entry changes.
- [ ] Do not chase a large gap-up or widen the stop.
- [ ] If conditions are not met, take no trade.

## After exit
- [ ] Record fill, exit, costs, slippage, holding sessions, and net P&L.
- [ ] Record whether rules were followed and what can be improved.
